What to check before ordering

An OrientDig spreadsheet helps you keep product links, size notes and price estimates together. Use the checklist below to decide which listings to keep on your shortlist.

Match the exact option

Save the seller link, color, size and date checked. For shoes, confirm the size system and whether the chart shows foot length or insole length. For clothing, compare flat garment measurements with something you own.

Review QC photos

If your agent supplies warehouse photos, compare the photographed option with your order. Ask for a close-up or ruler photo if a seam, outsole, label or measurement matters to your decision.

Check the exchange window

Confirm the deadline, return charges and steps before approving international dispatch. Save the listing and any measurement or exchange details you receive.

Shipping budget in USD

Enter the amounts from your agent’s quote. For international shipping, confirm the packed dimensions and ask which weight the carrier uses for billing. Include any applicable fees or import charges you expect to pay.

Frequently asked questions

Where do the products on this page come from?

The catalog shows selected CAFANS listings. Both main buttons open CAFANS, and each product name opens its listing. Check which shopping agents are available for that listing before you order.

What does the listed USD price cover?

It is the reference item price displayed on CAFANS. Your total can change with the selected option, exchange rate, shipping, agent charges and import costs.

What should I do if a product link stops working?

Open the matching CAFANS category and search for the item again. If you find a replacement listing, check the seller, size chart and exact color before adding it to your shortlist.
